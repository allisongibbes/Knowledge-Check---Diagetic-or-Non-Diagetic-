Knowledge Check: The Soundscape

Test your ear! Read each scenario below and determine the type of sound being used.

Scenario A
"A character puts on headphones, presses play, and blasts heavy metal music as they walk down the street."
Correct! This is Diegetic sound because the source of the music (the headphones) exists physically within the world of the story. The character can hear it just like we can.
Scenario B
"A slow, mournful violin plays as the camera pans across a deserted, snow-covered battlefield."
Correct! This is Non-Diegetic sound. There is no orchestra sitting in the middle of the battlefield; the music is added outside the story world purely to evoke an emotional response from the audience.
Scenario C
"We hear a loud, muffled heartbeat pounding rapidly while a terrified character hides in a closet from an intruder."
Correct! This is Point of Audition (also known as Internal Diegetic). The sound design has been modified to put us directly inside the subjective physical and psychological perspective of the character.
🎉 Great listening! You've mastered the terminology of the soundscape.
